About Ashish Barjatya

Experience, relationships and an intimate understanding of Indore.

Ashish Barjatya Associates is a founder-led real estate advisory built over two decades in Indore. We have grown alongside the city — from its first organised townships to today's Super Corridor and Indore–Ujjain growth axis — and have represented Omaxe and Godrej Properties through every stage of that journey.

Credibility in real estate is not claimed. It accumulates — through transactions that closed cleanly, documentation that was verified before commitment, and clients who came back and brought their families.

Local Expertise

Rooted in Indore.
Focused on what comes next.

Indore is the largest city in Madhya Pradesh and its commercial capital — home to IIT and IIM, and consistently ranked India's cleanest city. Its growth is moving along a handful of corridors, and every project we represent sits on one of them.

  • Super Corridor

    The city's principal westward growth axis, anchored by institutional and IT development.

  • Indore–Ujjain Road

    A fast-developing corridor with highway upgrades and planned metro connectivity.

  • Bypass Road & Mangliya

    Established connectivity supporting premium plotted and villa development.

  • Rau & Mayakhedi

    Consolidated residential belts with ready-to-possess inventory.
